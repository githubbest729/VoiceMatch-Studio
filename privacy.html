Privacy

VoiceMatch Studio is local-first. Everything you type stays in your browser.

  • No telemetry. No analytics, cookies, trackers or third-party scripts.
  • No servers. Drafts, banned words and profile details (including a photo) are stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • Backups are yours. Export and import produce plain JSON files on your device.
  • Deleting data. Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything.
